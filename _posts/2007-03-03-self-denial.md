Today is a very special day. And yet every day in life is special. It's just upto us, how we live it?  
It's a subtle phase of self denial. It's a unusual time of "need". There are so many things that I need right now. My life is one of them. It's not very often that I stop to think, where am I going?  
Thats the biggest problem with me. For all the enthusiasm that I have to keep moving, to keep afloat and to keep the pace, the biggest pitfall in my life is, my screwed up sense of direction. often I waste so much of effort and energy in re-tracing my steps back. If it wasn't myself, I would term the person as "moron" or maybe "looser". :)

Anyway, its one of those rarest days, when I stop to think in front of my cupboard mirror and shed some tears while reconciling my efforts, failures and shortcomings. I realize, that I have thrown away several offerings in recent past and I am still not sure, whether I should commend myself for this gutsy breakthrough for looking beyond the obvious and identifying a bait from an oppurtunity or should I just regret the most stupid thing that I have ever done in my life. Whatever it is, it's now a mere "experience". :)

I hope, I don't loose my enthusiasm and will to stay afloat because, I know in long run that is what will actually matter. :)  
Several people have turned me down today. From various perspectives. Few have tagged me as a irresponsible or unconcerned co-worker as well as a lousy friends and few have labelled me as a pointless acquaintance. Others, decided I was just nobody, that I was already scared, that I was. And eversince I have been caught up in whirlpool of depression.

Maybe I am not a people person and that explains that while whole world is partying the night out on an occasion of "festival colors" I am plugged into a machine, being not so different from itself. However music in my ears, says something very sensible right now :
