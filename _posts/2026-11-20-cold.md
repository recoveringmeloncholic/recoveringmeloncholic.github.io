It is 4:30 pm and the sun has already set. There is a call going on, I am listening passively. I have to visit a colleague at their house for dinner. But I feel like jumping into my night clothes, get into bed under blankets, watch the current series that I am watching on Netflix and order some shrimp thai red curry and sticky rice. I feel a bit sad inside, I don't know why. May it is the weather. Maybe it is the feeling of being loved. 

We are in November and in a festive part of the year for this geography. The streets are lit up and decorated, couples thronging the streets, holding hands, nuzzling into each other's coat. Today is even friday. I know how it will be. I'll step out, needle through the cloud, earphones plugged in and music drowning out the hubbub around. Feeling more alone than ever. 

I feel like, that feeling has become my identity now. When I stray far from it, I get nervous, like I am not home, in a foreign land. 

So, here is to forcing myself out of this warm apartment, into the chilly night to navigate the social norms and crowds that move like you don't exist. 

Don't even know what I am saying except maybe describing a feeling to myself.
