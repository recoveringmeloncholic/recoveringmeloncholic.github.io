Just when I thought I was feeling low enough, I saw few guys chasing down a pig on busy road, apparently because he seemed to have somehow escaped from their deathly gallows.  
He look scared as hell, and couldn't think of anything for nuts. "Civilized" traffic, gaping crowd and pack of ruthless hunters.  
Got caught eventually, picked up by neck, hair pulled and leg-dragged. It was nothing less than a butcher show in public.  
But then it proved one thing, "**In the land of pigs, Butcher is the King!**"

I felt so weak and lonely on his behalf and on my own.  
Since morning I have been reeling in quicksand of depression. I mean, take a good look at my life right now! Tell me what is it that I can do? Who is it that I can call? I mean, I am "ABSOLUTELY" what you call as "useless".

Went to office, because there is nothing at home to do and no where else to go. Came back to home because, don't feel like working and there is no one I could meet or spend time with. It really doesn't get anymore sad, and If I don't do something about it soon, it's gonna get me.

Fuck my misery... This dedication to that pig today, who might have lost his last bit while whole world looked on.  
I wish he revives again as a man to kill in least, those three pigs who snatched it all...
