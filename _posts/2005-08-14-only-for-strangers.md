And these are the fucking reasons why anyone would think i am "**WEIRD**"

- I feel a secret elation and happiness from within, when i am sad.
- I fancy a cute little daughter, whom i will adopt as i do not plan to get married. And will take care of her with all my heart and soul; while she will be sweet, smart, intelligent and cute. weird..?
- I fancy about a dog [black, labrador] with name "Joules" ... earlier it was "Ross" but i changed, after it conflicted with character in Friends. He is and will be my best pal.
- Sometimes, i strive to make myself sad.
- I have a longing to get slouched with liquor.
- Somewhere within, i have a feeling, i don't love my parents and sister, though everything that i am doing, is for them.
- I want someone to read all my poems, diaries and articles and tell me that they are nice and it's ok to be weird.
- I want to "HUMILIATE" few people in this world, who have made last 15 years as hell for me and my parents. I still can't get myself to do it.
- I know i am a misanthrope, i just somehow can't get myself to accept it.
- I have a secret desire to spread as much love as possible to all those, who run short of it in real life. Especially ones on "**[Post Secret](http://postsecret.blogspot.com/)**"
- I love my dad and want to tell him everything. But somehow my judgement is affected by my mom's advices and i don't do so. I have started to do so though.. i am trying.
- I WANT to be weird.
