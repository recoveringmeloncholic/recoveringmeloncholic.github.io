*So what's my story today?*  
Another circle of problems. You know, what's wrong in having all problems in a circle? "No matter what you are looking away from, you are still looking 'at' something". :)

I wrote whole lot of stuff, but deleted it. I don't even know what I wanna blog.  
There is so much to write, about how I hate my life once again. Because, I am on verge of losing my home. Because I am on verge of losing my sanity. Because I am on verge of losing of whatever little is left there for me and my family.  
I am like a fretting fish in the net! I have been crying in and out. But at the same time I am savoring each moment of passing agony; because when I take my revenge; I'll need it's vividness, for those fretting fishes once again!

God, I plead you on my knees; for one last time; don't take away our last bit from us. Please. This is all that we all have left!  
**And yet, even if you do; I promise on my soul, I'll build it all over again from scratch; but, then all that would be missing, would be faith in u!**

I mean it.  
I choose sleep over words today! Because, I need to leave this world. And thats the only way, I willing to do it right now. :)
