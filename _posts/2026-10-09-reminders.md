Woke up to the sound of waves. Today was a lot less chaotic than yesterday. I'd gone to see my dad before starting the trip early yesterday morning, and he told me he wasn't feeling well and needed to see a doctor. That put me in a tough spot: every booking for the trip was already locked in, I couldn't cancel on such short notice, and I also couldn't just leave my dad in unreliable hands. I took his temperature, gave him medicine. Looked like a viral infection. I told him to take his meds and turn in early.

We left as scheduled anyway, but spent the drive calling an agency, found someone who could stay home and help him, booked a doctor's appointment, arranged an auto to take him there and back, and ordered his medicine. Found someone else who could start early today and asked him to cover the next two to three days. All of this happening on the move, from inside the car, while my sister stayed on standby in case she needed to fly in. He still wasn't doing great by the end of the day, and I kept getting more worried, wondering if we'd need to turn back. But he woke up fine this morning, and he actually seems to be doing well. I'm relieved. Things feel back on track. That's put my mind at ease.

Through all of this, she's been sending me reminders for my medicine. Do I actually need them? Of course not. I'm a fully functioning ~adult~ _somewhat of an_ adult, perfectly capable of taking my own medicine without a nudge. Do I look forward to them anyway? Absolutely, I love them. It's less about the reminder itself and more that just getting a message from her makes me happy. No idea why. Today she seemed a bit low. I don't know how to help with that, and while boundaries were originally her idea, I'm the one trying harder to honor them these days. I hope she feels better and that her travel goes as smoothly as she said it would. I keep wondering if I should find time to check in and see how she's actually doing. What if I'm the problem, the reminders, the chatting, all of it? Maybe I should give her more space. Except she already has more than enough of it.

In other news, I still haven't managed to pick her blog back up. Haven't restarted from where I left off. Maybe I give it one more day. I don't know what kind of gremlins are waiting for me once I do. I'll admit, I'm a little scared.
