Woke up to the sound of waves. Today was a lot less chaotic than yesterday. The night day before, I'd gone to see my dad before starting the trip early yesterday morning, and his caretaker told me that he wasn't feeling well and needed to see a doctor. That put me in a tough spot: every booking for the trip was already locked in, I couldn't cancel on such short notice, and I also couldn't just leave my dad in unreliable hands, especially when caretaker was unwell. I took his temperature, gave him medicine. Looked like a viral infection. I told him to take his meds and turn in early.

We left as scheduled anyway, but spent the drive calling multiple agencies, finally around 2 pm found someone who could stay home and help the caretaker, booked a doctor's appointment, arranged an auto to take him there and back, and ordered his medicine. Found someone else who could start early today and asked him to cover the next two to three days. All of this happening on the move on a 14 hour drive, from inside the car, while my sister stayed on standby in case she needed to fly in. The caretaker still wasn't doing great by the end of the day, and I kept getting more worried, wondering if we'd need to turn back. But he woke up fine this morning, and he actually seems to be doing well. I'm relieved. Things feel back on track. That's put my mind at ease.

Through all of this, she's been sending me reminders for my medicine. Do I actually need them? Of course not. I'm a fully functioning _somewhat of an_ adult, perfectly capable of taking my own medicine without a nudge. Do I look forward to them anyway? Absolutely, I love them. It's less about the reminder itself and more that just getting a message from her makes me happy. No idea why. Today she sounded a bit low. I don't know how to help with that, and while strong boundaries were originally her idea, I'm the one trying harder to honor them these days. I hope she feels better and that her travel goes as smoothly as she said it would. I keep wondering if I should find time to check in and see how she's actually doing. What if I'm the problem, the reminders, the chatting, all of it? Maybe I should give her more space. Except she already has more than enough of it. What am I suppose to do, I suppose just be nearby and thats all. How am I to play the two opposite roles at the same time? I think, no one can. 

In other news, I still haven't managed to pick her blog back up. Haven't restarted from where I left off. Maybe I give it one more day. I don't know what kind of gremlins are waiting for me once I do. I'll admit, I'm a little scared.

Last night I had a dream after a long time, where she was involved in some way. We are in this residential community of sorts. Things are happening. Some car crashes and other eventful stuff that I don't remember clearly. Some one is asking that I should call them out by name when something occurs (hazy details). I tell them I'll call you by "firebush" (_in the dream it was the real name of firebush_). They look at me in. a puzzled manner. My wife gives me a sideward glance. She knows that name. Few minutes later something else happens and I yet again tell them that I'll call them out by _her_ name. Things are awkward enough now, everyone wondering what is my obsession with the name? 

When I got up, everything was blurry, except this part of dream stayed behind. It was like I was trying to integrate her forcefully into my life. 

I find myself thinking about her all the time and I am still not sure why and what am I thinking. Maybe nothing, maybe in that tiny world inside my head where there are no rules, expectations or right and wrong, I have the freedom to do as I will. Nothing has to make sense, it has to just feel good. That is the place my mind drifts to when I go to sleep often now. Once again.
