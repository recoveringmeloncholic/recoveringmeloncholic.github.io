Today I delivered my report, the third in a row. Weeks of toil, stitched together with long travel, early mornings and late nights. My body is spent, my mind a little numb, and I feel like a hamster on a wheel, running and running, pacing up, with no idea toward what.

One more week of this remains. So much has happened in three weeks that I don't know what to recount. I'll tell what I can and let the rest go, the way we must let go of some things in life.

**First,** four straight weeks of crushing work. My to-do lists sat untouched. Most days I panicked, and my mind lived in a hurry. Even my reading turned hurried, because hurry had become the default way I moved through the world. That is not good for the soul. It forgets how to pause.

**Second,** weeks of anxious, back-to-back calls with customers, often unprepared and without enough support. Too much work, too many things happening at once. I kept up somehow, and kept dropping things along the way. Somehow I am here, and I am glad that I am.

**Third,** the long flight. I travelled over the weekend and didn't sleep a minute in the air. When I landed, it was raining. I usually take the express train from the airport, but this time I chose a taxi. The cold was fierce, yet the moment I got in, warmth found me, and loneliness right behind it. So far from home, alone in this city, I usually feel it most.

**Fourth,** the mornings begin at 4 am. They always have on these visits, but I used to step out to run and enjoy the park. This time I was on calls from 4 am, every day. That ends tomorrow. The first call is at seven, and I can sleep late.

**Fifth,** a cringe-worthy session. I was supposed to talk about my growth journey. Everyone told me to share a little background and let people see what I have done. I took a leap of faith and put myself out there. I spoke of things that felt personal and uncomfortable, hoping to show some people the person behind the role. Somewhere along the way I ended up oversharing. Someone told me in passing that it sounded like a therapy session. I was mortified. I cursed myself the whole evening for opening up, for being vulnerable in front of the whole office. I couldn't sleep last night, angry at myself, maybe even disappointed, because this was possibly the first time I had put down my guarded professional armour and tried to be my more authentic self. It was a stupid idea. Unless someone comes along and tells me otherwise.

**Sixth,** the final report presentation, today, to the group I find hardest to face. Questions thrown like stones, confrontation, aggressive pushback. We fielded it all, and somehow came out on top. That was the relaxing moment at last.

**Finally,** I look around and see so many problems I have yet to solve. I feel overwhelmed. I don't know how I will cross this chasm. This week my confidence took its biggest hit. I will treat it with time, and by slowing down. Sleep, walk, exercise, slow down. That is the plan.

In all of this I never found a moment to come here and pour out what I felt each day. Some days were distress, some sorrow, some hope. Most nights I fell asleep longing to be held and loved. Today I ache to love someone, in body and in mind. I think it grounds a person, and my body is craving ground. I have no way to satisfy that, except to sleep it off. I don't want to make poor decisions or say things that carry consequences for me and for others. I want to find my balance and carry on with this journey called life.

Before this trip, on the morning of my flight, I finished my will. I sent a signed copy to my wife by email and WhatsApp. I can't say why, but at the start of the trip I felt I wouldn't make it home, and I wanted to leave her paperwork that would clear the way. That was the first piece of work I had signed up for, done.

My sister wants us to take a trip together. I've told her January. Let's see how it goes. Some days, when I am away from home, I miss my kids and my wife. I know very well that the moment I am home, the feeling will dissolve.

Yesterday I was so unsettled that, for the first time in years, I hummed a song while walking down the road. Mostly to drown out the storm raging in my mind. The song was [Wonderwall by Oasis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bx1Bh8ZvH84).

So this is a tangle of thoughts, not yet in order. More coherent ones will come later. I miss being loved, and loving back. My wife tells me she loves me, and I tell her too, though not always. But I am not sure we mean the same thing, or mean it in the same way.
