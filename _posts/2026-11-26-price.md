Everything has a price.

Have kids, and you pay in chaos. If you don't, then you pay it with the family you never get to raise. Live alone, and you pay in loneliness. Live with family, and you pay with space that is never yours.

Nothing in life is free. We only get to choose which cost we are willing to pay.

So I ask myself what is wrong with the price I have chosen to pay. I have two beautiful kids. I have a partner who is supportive, who co-parents well and shares the weight of the daily grind. What I don't have is a real physical and emotional relationship.

When the night goes quiet and the day has grown heavy, that absence weighs down on me. Otherwise, life goes on, as it must, until it can't.

If I die first, she will light me up and I'll be dust. If she goes first, I'll do as she wills, and live out the rest of my days the way I live them now. In either case, we would leave behind two beautiful souls, hopefully.

Would that really be so bad?

The only thing that throws all of this into disarray is a love that feels so within reach. Yet it keeps blinding me to the price I would have to pay for it, a price I know I'll refuse when the time comes to pay. Then why do I keep torturing myself with it? I should focus on the here and now, and make the best of what I have. No?

I ask again, would that really be so bad?
