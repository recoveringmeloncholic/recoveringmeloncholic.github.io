Listening to [this](https://youtu.be/6QDcxBhqD5k) since morning today. It brings a certain calm and peace that was absent for the whole week. 

I say, I have learnt we have no control over life, and that I need to be patient and then the first thing I do is "_plan_" and set "_timelines_". The sheer dichotomy and irony of that.

> We wrestle with nature, seek love all around,  
> Yet love is not a thing to be found.  
> It reveals itself when we seek it least,  
> Creeping upon us, a stealthy beast.  
>   
> When lost in life, we let ourselves go,  
> And sink into depths we never did know.  
> Don’t seek the shape that you’ve wrought,  
> Love is a drifting cloud, a form sought not.    
>  
> Fleeting or permanent, a tempest to cross,  
> Warm as an embrace, yet wrenching as a loss.  

Would it be so bad if I just died today? Would I regret anything in life? When I ask that question, I am a happy man, not because I have everything, but because I feel content with everything I have.
