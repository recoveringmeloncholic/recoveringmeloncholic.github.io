And it's a dream. Or maybe it's not. It's so hard to differentiate anyway. In between stepping out of dream world and stepping back into it; you live reality somewhere in between. And yet you stay so oblivious to it. But I guess, the reality marks it's presence with the depressing lurch and free fall feeling that it brings. And before the blood in your veins succumbs to the ultimate tact of depression; illusions recoil the feeling and pull you up.

Eventually; it becomes the rise and fall of king everyday.  Because if in your own realm you are not the king; who else is? Every single day faith and trust unites and fortifies. But the devil of depression and suspicion corrodes it away. And once again I am left wondering and unsure looking for assurances and clues of love that I long for so intensely.

For a month now; it is just a echo that is left. I feel; we humans are so pathetic. Greedy evil creatures who take anything and everything for granted; comfortably ignoring the immense sacrifice and effort behind it. And yet I cannot make up my mind; because I do not know which is better or worse? Knowing that you can never be loved and living with it; like there was no end to world. Or getting love that you always wanted and knowing it's fading away or at least with each passing moment "it might be!".

I hate suspense. I hate uncertainty. I wanna know for sure! Always.

Preaching for hours; how you can live and fight against depression; I know the truth in it's entirety that I "love" depression and can't live without it.  
I know for sure; I love you. But I know at the same time; it's all in my head. Maybe one of them is eternal truth but I am not ready to find out. I am scared of the answer I don't wanna hear.

To be honest; I am the biggest coward there ever was. :'(  
Low you. Right now that is the only truth for me. And I am insecure and scared about you. Please don't leave me.  
~Me
