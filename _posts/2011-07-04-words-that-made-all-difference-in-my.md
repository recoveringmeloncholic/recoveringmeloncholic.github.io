I was there, doing a posting on Craiglist. I got responses and I think at least in my mind, I was hooking up with someone for a night. Absurdly I convinced myself it was ok. She would be ok with it.  
While I did stuff, I was listening songs on Grooveshark. One of the songs that got queued up, was "What are words" by Chris Medina. It's a nice song, but there was a line in there, that made the whole difference to me.

It just said; "Cause, what kind of guy would I be. If I was to leave when you need me most".

I think; that just made me think; if thats all I really thought was to relationships. If thats only how much I valued her? If thats the kind of a guy I was. I had dreamt that there was a tragic event in my stay and I was never going to see her. It was a horrible dream. When I was posting stuff on craiglist for NSA or one night stands; I was thinking; hopefully I won't get trapped into some kind of stupid thing; wondering if my dream would come true.

And then I just had that epiphany; my intention of hooking up for one night was the tragic event. That was the lowest level in my life I have ever been. I really missed her at that point and realized how dangerous loneliness is.  This is not who I am.

I deleted the post; I deleted the emails. I apologized to everyone and replied to their emails; that it wasn't happening. And even though I lost some respect for myself; it was the best decision I made.

It would have been ironical; as our first encounter was based on a petty promise I didn't fulfill. And I was all ready out here to end my rock solid next 60 years prematurely. How the FUCK could I do that.

Thanks Chris for saving me and from humiliating myself in my own eyes. I owe you one for this song. One of the part that I really in this song.
