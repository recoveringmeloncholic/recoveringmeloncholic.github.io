**Reclaim your life?**  
Probably much easier said than done. My first question would be; Are you living only for yourself? Are there no obligations to people who mean to you so much, whatsoever? Are you thinking about their life, while you try to set yourself on a quest, so called **reclaim-your-life**?  
If you think, that world is all about you, as you are the **alpha and omega** this world serves a purpose for and you are the only reason that everyone is what everyone is; while others are no more significant than a forgotten dream of a sleepless night; then i would say, sure go ahead and "**re-claim**" your "**so-called-life**".  
You are no better than a **flourescent colour**, which looks meaningful only in a TV commercial promoting a equally meaningless product of a worthless  
conglomerate; you are destined to grab all the attention but only until some other jazzy combination "reclaims" that attention.  
Life is not about reclaiming it for induced implusivness of 2 minutes, which lasts as a regret of lifetime, life is not about "wearing your attitude" and a condom, life is not about running and playing football on a steel frame built several feet above the ground, life is not about harleys, black smoke and skid marks, life is not about playing it cool, life is not about making it large or making it big. Life is not **ONLY** about "**chasing ONLY your dreams**".  
Life is about chasing and realizing someone else's dream for them. Thats when you become all that you ever wanted to be. Thats when you make it large and thats when "**you are the man**". **Selflessness**, as the whole idea is potrayed conventionally; has become a word people have gotten over with time. **World is lost. I am lost too.** So who will save us now? Who will save "**ME**" now? [sigh... I have just become one of those, "**don't follow, what you preach**" people.]
