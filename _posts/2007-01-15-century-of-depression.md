This is my 100th post! It has taken  almost two years or little less to reach this mark.  The time is significant but what is more significant, is the  evolution that this blog captures!  
If I scroll down really fast, it almost feels as if I am running down memory lane.

I know most of these blogs were written when I was drunk. Because when I am not, either I am too bored to write one or I just dont have anything to write. More importantly, they reflect true me and pure truth.  
Only I know, how many beads of water have run down those cheeks while these words were knit together.
