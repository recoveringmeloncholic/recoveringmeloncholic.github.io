I read on the wall: **"Life is calling where are you?"**

*"I am decaying in dungeons of obligations and responsibilities tied in shackles of limitations and small thinking."*

Few days back that would have been my answer. But I have decided to quit.  
Quit from **depression**,  
Quit from **obssession**,  
Quit from **orthodoxy**,  
Quit from **lax**,  
Quit from **quitting**.

Insight that I have gained in past few days, into my own life, is worth anything.  
I know I don't love her. It's just an infatuation to the role, she played. And that can be anyone. Even now. And thats, whats more important  
I know there is no formidable pressure in my life; and that way I am blessed than most of the people, who are quashed against deadlines to save lives of loved ones.  
I know the "elevator" of my life is going up. The falls are yet to come. The most important thing is to not get off from elevator, while it's going down. All it takes to change course, is "pushing the right button" and pressing that button is "all upto me". And now onwards I am game for it.

The strand of sorrow stricken post will cease to exists, on this blog now onwards.  
I am willing to surender my pessimistic perspective, In hope to capture the challenges of life in their true spirit and showcase my will to go on in it's deserved frame of respect.

Here on this blog shall sing of dreamt glory and steps that I'll take to reach my goals!  
Adieu, my insane soul.

Dedication to this moment, are following few lines; I wrote on a railway track; on my journey back to city where I found and lost it all.

All holes healed  
all regrets forsaken  
all mistakes forgiven  
and no offence taken  
I stand here to begin new story  
standing grinning amidst my dreamt glory  
I shall begin again, once and for all  
Not crippled by fear, not afraid to fall.  
Ready to evince the power of "One"  
**hold on crazy world, here I come!**
