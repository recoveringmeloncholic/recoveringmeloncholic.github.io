My knuckle is healing. It doesn't pain anymore. I am not sure if that's a good thing. Till now it kept reminding me of her. Anyway, what's the point? As everyone has already told me, that hanging on and trying still, is an unwise and stupid thing to do. But then, something inside, keeps asking me; "*just because it would make you look unwise and stupid; would you choose to not do it"*? I really don't know the answer. Probably, I would not do it because, I know she doesn't have much time left to get over me and all that is about me. I can still give myself years to move on; but we are not sure, how many weeks she has got! And I thought, life was being tough on me! Yeah.. Right!?  
**"Plum", Don't keep hanging; just let go. Please. I am not worth it anyway.**
