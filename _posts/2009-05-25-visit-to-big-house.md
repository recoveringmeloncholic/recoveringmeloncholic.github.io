It was a wierd wierd dreams, but then which ones are not? :)

Before anything here is a small bit that I remember and which doesn't make any sense. There is a guy playing something on stereo in uncle's verenda towards road. I went there and there is this magical kind of things; where I am seeing either a alternate reality or something illusional. Can't recall what I saw but it was crazy. And then someone came next to me and it was over. There was lot more to that part but I can hardly recall anything.

How it began I don't clearly remember. But I remember that I found myself in a large house somewhere as there was some cause for it. There was some marriage like function. Polls were going on in city.

Some friends and some other school mates were there in that house. There was also a frined of a friend, cousin of my roommate and few other random people. As I write it, it all seems to be quickly evaporating.

There was a chemistry lecture being organized in house. I don't know if I had paid for it because it felt like that I did. but then I didn't want to attend in least and yet I think I had paid around 12k for it. Lots of other guys were trying to join that as everyone said; it was a great lecture.

I was trying to find R. My mom was also there in the house; so obviously I wanted to meet her without her seeing me.

We all went out for walk; can't exactly remember who all were there but we had some hostel mates and YES! Obama with me and we were walking down from a muddy mountain. It was really high, we were towards the ground. And that mountain had strange things potruding out of it. A U shape like stone formation. which was about 5 feet thick and people were walking on it from top of the hill. Like ants. Thats what I thought, that we always complain about ants moving like that on all things; now look at us. Someone had a foot ball up there; which they let go and bumping around it eventually came down to us.

We played for a bit and then we were back in the big hall of that house. I was sitting and wondering something when that chemistry professor came and put nice plum cakes, custurds etc on table. I helped myself. When he came back he was surprised that I ate it because it was only for people he was going to teach upstairs. I profousley apologized as I didn't realize it was for selective people and he was going to take it upstairs.

I took off from there to find R in house. I remember that at some point I felt that I should go back and go to my house (at that point I had an image of house in ██████████ and uncle) and also wanted to just lie in bed and look up at cieling and think nothing or something.

Finally I went about rooms searching for mine. I saw a friend sleeping in her room and my roommate was sleeping in mine on one bed and another bed was probably empty for me. But my mom was going towards that room and thats why I went to friend's room and hid in her bathroom. I could see through the grill. With some noise that I made, friend got up, adjusted her clothes and I whispered to her through grill. She came to bathroom door pushed it and when we was entering from the little crack which had formed because I had let the door be ajar, but only a bit; she gave me a quick peck on lips. I was shocked and said, never do that again. And at the same time to not make her uncomfortable, I hugged her, don't ever do that again. She said, okie and I asked her to find R and get something from her for me. My mom was in next room and was already trying to find me through my roommate.

Friend went to get that stuff. Meanwhile I eventually came to the balcony where roommate was lying in morning sunlight on a slanting bed kind of thing; to which I thought. How come he always manages to stay in best of placed or rooms. And where did he get that bed from? Did he get that himself? Or did he find it in house?

His cousin was standing there and they were trying to make a phone call. but their phone wouldn't work. So he asked my phone and since I had point UI, to show that he can't work his way through he tried to operate it with his nose and laughed. I showed him how to make a call and got out of there.

I also found that I was trying to stand in elections and there was an option for me. It was quite simple I think. This part again gets hazy but eventually I saw so much riots in city; there was small fire in all the places and I saw in news paper that there will be 16,500 participants and elite group will be formed out of it. And with that I withdrew as I didn't feel I'll be able to make a difference with that. It was just a joke with people. There was a specific party which was trying to lure with some tactics but can't recall now.

All in all, I didn't get to see R no matter how I tried and I don't remember most of it, but it was surely a long long one. :)
