Sometimes, life isn't any better than "crack". You feel inordinately high, but you know slowly it's killing you inside.

Wise things that I learned today:

- You should never miss a good chance to shut up
- To the questions of your life, you are the answer and to the problems of you life, you are the solution
- Albert einstein was the second genius after Bill Watterson. Because now that i think about it; "Three laws of gravity" has never helped me live my life any better than the feeling of knowing that if I don't lick my ice cream off the edges of cone, it'll aim down for earth; proving to be a waste for my "hard earned" money. But those monochromatic strips of pure humor by bill watterson has not only made me feel relaxed day after day, but also made me accept facts of life in most ingenious of ways. It is bill watterson who made a difference and not Albert. I am quite sure on that point.
- Nike sucks. They are nothing but a conglomerate who charges people to wear their name printed on a cheap fabric. They should be banned.
- Reality will continue to ruin your life. And that is the ultimate truth. As long as you are fine with the idea. **You are the MAN!**
- No one is obliged to love you. Not even you yourself.
- Making sacrifices for other people is not worth. Not only they deny to realize it, they also hold you responsible for being the selfish one. [I say, you might as well be.. :-| ]
- No other feeling is better than, finding a lost friend back into your life. You have a reassurance, that often you can count on people, you thought you could count on. **D.P.** this is learned from you. I am so much happy that we are friends again. Good to have you back.
- Things done in a spur of moment are best as they inject happiness like nothing else. Atleast you are happy for those few moments. And that is what life is all about. Being happy. If you would be reckless whole of your life, probably you would be always happy! :-D
- always post your lessons; someone might be reading and "learning" too!
