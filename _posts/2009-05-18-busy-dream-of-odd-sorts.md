It's a hazy feeling sort of now. The dream has passed away and I really remember only bits and pieces. There were too many of them intermingled. But I'll try to recall whatever I can and will document here.

I remember seeing lots of diamonds or stones. This probably came in my dream because yesterday I was talking to R how I would buy her a diamond ring if she would unintentionally slip me her finger size. :) So in this dream we were sitting in a big hall or something (something from Underworld-Rise Of Lycans movie that I watched just before sleeping) and we were talking of diamonds etc and there were so many of them.

How it transitions to another one I don't know but I saw that we were trying to get cousins's admission done in some specific school and for that we were trying to fill forms and get things in place. However one thing that wasn't falling in place was insurance of his foot. (Came from the talk I had with parents about insurance, as how office covers dental and medical insurance both). Cousin was having a severe foot pain and we were wondering how to get the covered and treated. I wore the same shoes and realized that it has a pin in it.

We wanted to show that to principal and put up a notice but she had removed the notice and put up another one regarding the results of some sorts. One thing we all kept pondering was that the pin was poking straight from the shoes while the pain was in the curve of the front side of foor, but we were sure it's because of that pin. And I think we wanted to show that pin because only seeing that pin, principal would have approved getting insurance for that.

From that part my mind wandered to some small mountains which were made of vitrified tiles, chips and other sorts of flooring. A cousin and a friend were climbing those mountains and I was trying to stop them. Uncle eventually shouted and call them back. While cousin came back jumping appropriatly, my friend missed a level and jumped a height of about two floors. She landed safely and grinned about missing the level. :| I was furious and said, are you alright? Have you gone mad jumping from such heights?

There was also some biking involved in mountains but I can't remember that part clearly. It was a busy time in dream. All the time I was upto something or another.

But thats all I can recall now. I lied in bed thinking about it and recalling but alas, not much comes back to you once you open your eyes. :) Only if I could blog about it with my eyes closed.
