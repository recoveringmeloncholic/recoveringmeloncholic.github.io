There is a general ennui in the air. I woke up unwilling to acknowledge the clock. I wanted to lie in bed with my eyes closed and think about life and love, not looking for an answer or an epiphany. The cycle has turned enough times that I know what I really want, and I know what I will do about it, so there's nothing left to guess at or figure out. Maybe it was yet another acceptance of that reality that made me feel this way.

I will be sad and low for a while. Then I'll talk myself up, push myself to do better. I'll start exercising again, find hobbies and interests, get back on track. And one day it will all go haywire again. That is life in a nutshell.

For now I am sifting through new songs, looking for one to hold on to for the next few days. I already have one that takes me back to walking the immaculate streets of London in the winter chill. Indexing one memory at a time.

I need a drive, a long one, by myself. To sit with this ennui and grow more comfortable with it instead of resisting it. The upcoming office travel might help.

Or maybe I'll skip the drive and go to dad's house instead, order far too much unhealthy food, and watch Netflix until I doze off. Repeat this gluttnous cycle until I depise myself and kick into the health overdrive.
