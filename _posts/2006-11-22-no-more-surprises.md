Past is so vivid sometimes. It'll chase you, it'll knock you down and then sit on you ridiculing your luck!  
I am not sure if I am obliged to do anything; but I guess it really doesn't matter. Truth is I am not sure of anything. I have spent so much time thinking what is right and what is wrong. But I know I don't want to find out anymore. I am, just what I am. So I'll always be.

When I got the mail saying, "I am looking forward that you'll land and surprise me!", all I could say was "Sorry,  no more surprises for you, anymore!" :)

I am not sure, If I can handle any more rejection. What you did to me, would be enough for lifetime. And yet, thanks for showing me the "either" side of life, people and love!
