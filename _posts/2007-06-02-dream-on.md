I am not drunk and still blogging! Thats pretty much an occasion! :) Mellow music is flexing my ear drums and I am half dazed. Lost and lost again. Because I think I have given up on people for once and all.

What have I become? I have absolutely no idea. Evil? Good? Better? Worse? I think I have just become prisoner of my own freedom.  Or may be I am just trying to be too much philosophical.

Not much to look up in life. I hope I live up to those last few things that I wanted to do in this so much wasted life. It's a sheer pity the kind of pathetic waste it has become. I am tired of trying to motivate myself; because people are ready to break that spell; the moment I cast it.

Finally, relentless crying has brought me back my whatever little sanity that I still have left. I wish something could bring back time and people who left me behind. In any case, hope you all live all the way to your dreams.

So long.
