There are two kinds of people: those who run from their past like their life depends on it, and those who cling to it like it's all they have. I want to be the first kind. I'm clearly the second.

Today is the 11th of September. One of the most beautiful days of my life, and nothing I ever say or do will change that.

I found someone I genuinely love. I learned to love myself. I learned more about the world and about people.

Wherever I end up, I won't regret this. I remember the panic attack I induced in you before that flight took off. I don't think we knew what we sounded like until this day? No? One of my nicest memory. (_endearing kiss on the back of the palm_)

Sometimes, I think I would message you and check in how you are doing. But by now I know my patterns and I don't think they help anyone. I don't think I will, as harsh as it may seem. But I really hope you are well and taking care of yourself.
