"**So whatchya gonna do anyway? :)**"

Deliberated carefully for 32 minutes and 43 seconds, whether I should smoke or not?  
Standing in front of that lull grey "**not-so-tall**" refrigerator, with eyes focused behind that supid bonzai plant. Thats the place where my "**sticks of momentary salvation**" resided with total obliviousness about their obejctive of existence.  
But then, before I call them "oblivious and ignorant" and be a hypocrite; I must answer  it to myself and you, whether I am aware of my sole purpose of this mundane and puny [atleast, thats what it looks like] life??

And then, like always, this voice pops up in head once again, with one of it's rhetoric curt statements... "So whatchya gonna do anyway?"...

So.. I was like... yeah! Fuck it.. Just smoke, get high and sleep. Lets get done with it. To top it up, a vauge memory of book called "**Screw It, Lets do it**" clouded in my thought-chain!

It's so, amusing, that I often find "Motivation" for all the wrong things in life...  
Anyway, before I could kiss that "butt" of slim and provocative dose of cancer-wrapped stimulant; one of my friend called. So next 13 minutes and 24 seconds were spend in "why-are-you-so-low" conversation, which ended like always, at "Nowhere".

Anyway.. I think the conversation served it's purpose, cause I didn't smoke; and yet it left a lingering question in my mind...  
"Are you serving your purpose in life?"

Before I answer that... the elf in me says...  
**So whatchya gonna do anyway?**

5 minutes later I found myself replying... "I am gonna Fuck you and get on with my life!"  
And thats exactly what I am trying to do, today morning!

Good morning world!
