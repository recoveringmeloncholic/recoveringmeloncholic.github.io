> **Technetium: The Element That Wasn’t Supposed to Exist**

> I recently learned about Technetium, which sounds less like a chemical element and more like a startup founded by three engineers in a garage. Its claim to fame? Technetium was the first element to be artificially produced. Scientists had been looking for it for ages, but unlike most elements, it basically said, “You want me? Make me yourself.” Even more amusingly, Technetium has no stable isotopes, meaning every atom of it is radioactive and eventually disappears. It is essentially the chemical equivalent of a guest who arrives at a party, hangs around for a bit, and then quietly ceases to exist. Despite this existential commitment, Technetium is actually quite useful; especially in medical imaging, where radioactive technetium compounds help doctors see what’s happening inside the human body, and apparently Thyroid from personal experience!
