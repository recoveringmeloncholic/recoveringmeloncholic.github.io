Last night was a blur. I was crossing countries, talking to her on the phone, and the moment I boarded the flight I fell asleep. I think I dreamt of her somewhere over the clouds. Then a whole day of cars and flights, and finally the bed, at the end of a long, tired day.

But jet lag had found me, and in the dark I was thinking of her. I tried to follow my breath down into sleep. Somewhere along the way it slipped from my breathing to her, though I had told my mind to stay away.

We were in her house, in her bed. Our heads on the pillow, facing each other, noses nuzzling at the tip. I could feel her warm breath on my lips. We were talking about something, and I was drawn toward her. All night I lived in a strange in-between, not sleeping, and yet awake and lying next to her. I could tell when morning came. When the alarm rang, I felt I had never slept at all, and she was gone. It was as if we had talked until dawn. It was unlike any dream I know. It was as if we were plugged into each other's minds, intertwined. Everything blurred at the edges but never black. Not asleep, not awake. I can't describe it.

In the dream I wanted to tell her that I love her, and that I miss her every single day. I miss her physically and mentally. I think about things she would whisper to me in my ears. Word by word. I repeat those to myself, reminding of an exquisite love that washed over me. That I am dying to be touched, and to touch her. But I held back. I told myself she needs to find her own way, and I need to find mine. I don't want any artificial pressures. If tomorrow I decide the best course is to make my married life work, I don't want to feel I gave that choice away. I want to keep the freedom to do the right thing when the time comes. And still the pull of her scent and her warm breath kept drawing me toward her.

I woke groggy and a little disappointed, maybe. I longed to be wrapped up and held. But I got over it, stood under a hot shower, and felt normal again. I took back the command of my mind and body that my tired mind had let slip.

I am trying to talk to my wife about many things. I have brought some humour back into our days. I am realising that without fun and jokes, you can never truly like the person beside you. She tries to meet me there. She was always funny. We just lost it a long time ago. She called a while back and we gossiped for fifteen minutes, the first time in many months, maybe the first time this year. I felt conflicted, but I'm glad I held my tongue these last few days.

This is the push and pull I live in, the love of two people. One is easy to love, and one is easy to live with. And I end up choosing the one who is easy to live with.

Still, I missed her all day long. I need to sleep, and win back a little hold on my heart before it starts to drift away again.

_Umma! I do miss you, whatever said and done_
