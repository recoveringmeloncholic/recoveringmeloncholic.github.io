I have documented my life to a greater extent on this blog, especially during most of the depressing times. Here on I am going to convert it into a Dream diary, hence for my absolute personal use to maintain all the vivid dreams that I encounter now and then, lest they will be forgotten for ever.

Somehow I have this notion, that anything gained in this life should not be forgotten but documented, archived and preserved to reflect back on, in my old age when I'll have nothing but days to count.

Cheers to dream diary, which will serve a purpose of entertainment one day.
